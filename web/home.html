The voice agent you can
interrupt mid-sentence.

Today's assistants listen, think, speak, then act, one step at a time. Say “Wait, make it the 6pm flight” and they ignore you, freeze, or book both. CHRONOS treats every interruption as a new version of the plan, so stale work is cancelled and nothing is booked twice.

The core idea

Reads can be speculative. Writes must be committed.

While you are still talking, CHRONOS already searches flights and plans routes: those reads are safe to throw away. Anything that changes the world (booking, navigation, payment) waits until you have finished, and is re-checked against the current epoch right before it runs.

Interrupt, and the epoch moves on. Old tasks are cancelled, late results are dropped, and a booking that already went through is undone by a compensating action.

Where it matters

Four situations, one mechanism

In the car

“Navigate to Chennai Airport… actually, gas station first.” One route is set, the old one is cancelled.

Customer support

“Book a flight to Delhi tomorrow… next week instead.” No double booking or charge; a booking already made is refunded.

Field troubleshooting

Send a camera frame and ask what is wrong. The diagnosis is grounded in what the camera sees and a knowledge base.

Accessibility

“I want to boo… um… book a table for four.” Hesitation is not acted on, and self-corrections patch the task.
